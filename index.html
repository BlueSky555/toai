About TOAI

Uniting Minds
Across the Turkic World

The Turkic Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence is the flagship academic competition for students and researchers from Turkic-speaking nations — spanning Turkey, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, and the wider Turkic diaspora.

Participants tackle real-world problems in machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics — judged by a panel of world-renowned academics and industry leaders.

Beyond competition, TOAI is a platform for scientific exchange, mentorship, and the cultivation of a pan-Turkic AI research community that can compete and collaborate at the highest global levels.

The 2026 edition, hosted in İstanbul, marks the most ambitious edition yet — with expanded tracks, an industry exhibition, and a new junior category for students under 18.

Key Dates

2026 Schedule

Day 1
Arrival

Arrival & Accreditation, Opening Ceremony

Delegation registration, ID verification, kit distribution, official inauguration, and participant orientation.

Day 2

Competition Round I

A six-hour intensive programming round featuring 2 to 3 complex tasks. Participants must analyze data and develop algorithmic solutions within a pre-specified, secure software environment.

Day 3

Cultural & Academic Program

Workshops, campus tours, networking, and cultural exchange building bridges across the Turkic world.

Day 4

Competition Round II

A second six-hour session focused on applied AI and data science challenges. Evaluations are conducted via a private leaderboard to test model robustness and final solution accuracy.

Day 5

Jury Deliberation & Award Ceremony

Morning scoring, verification, and results compilation followed by afternoon medal presentations and closing remarks.

Day 6

Departure

Delegates check out and depart, concluding the official programme.

Host City

İstanbul,
Turkey

İstanbul — where continents meet, empires have risen, and innovation has never stopped. The host venue for TOAI 2026 is Yıldız Technical University (YTÜ), a historic and highly prestigious institution that perfectly blends the city's rich heritage with cutting-edge research and modern technology.

From the Bosphorus to Beyoğlu, delegates will experience a city whose history stretches millennia and whose future is written in code. Cultural excursions, city tours, and a closing gala on the waterfront are included in the official programme.

Travel grants and accommodation support are available for qualifying delegations from eligible nations.
